Why switch

When people move from Warp to Bonk

  • No account, no cloud. Warp ties features to sign-in and cloud sync; Bonk works offline with credentials in the Keychain.
  • Full SSH client. Host manager, visual SFTP, port forwarding and jump hosts in-app — not ssh one-liners.
  • Open source. MIT license; you can inspect exactly what your terminal does.
  • Privacy. No AI analysis of your command history; your shell stays yours.
  • Serial + Quake. Hardware debugging and global dropdown terminal, neither of which Warp offers.
Migration

Move from Warp in 5 minutes

  1. 1

    Download Bonk and drag it into Applications.

  2. 2

    Import your SSH config: File → Import SSH Config (reads ~/.ssh/config).

  3. 3

    Add your private key in Bonk; it is stored in the macOS Keychain.

  4. 4

    Connect, then open SFTP / port forwarding from the toolbar.

  5. 5

    Save your layout as a Workspace for one-click restarts.

FAQ

Common questions

  • Does Bonk have AI like Warp? No built-in AI. If privacy matters more than AI autocomplete, that is the point.
  • Does Bonk require an account? No. Download, connect, done.
  • Can I use Bonk as my local shell? Yes, it is a full terminal emulator, but the focus is remote work.
  • Is my data stored in the cloud? No. Credentials stay in the macOS Keychain.
